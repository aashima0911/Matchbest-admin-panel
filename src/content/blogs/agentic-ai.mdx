### Threats in AI Generation
- With the ability to create realistic media comes the necessity for robust security and ethical guardrails:
- Digital Identity Theft: The risk of deepfake technology being used for impersonation or misinformation.
- Intellectual Property (IP) Governance: Ensuring that AI-generated content respects copyright and maintains a clear data lineage.
- Governance & Compliance: Automating regulatory document analysis to ensure all AI-produced media complies with evolving global standards.

### Securing Your Creative Workflow
- To maintain trust and quality, Xelta.ai advocates for an Agentic Governance model:
- Enforce Data Guardrails: Automate access controls and rate limits to track data usage across your entire creative workflow.
- Continuous Evaluation: Use AI "judges" and human feedback to measure the quality and authenticity of every generated asset.
- Responsible AI Implementation: Ensure all generated voices and faces are used according to privacy regulations and ethical standards.

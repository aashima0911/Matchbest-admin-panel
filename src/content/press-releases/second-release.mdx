[Gurugram, India | January 2026] - MatchBest Group  announced the launch of Xelta, a powerful AI-driven creative platform designed to transform how individuals and businesses produce digital content. Built for creators, marketers, entrepreneurs, and design teams, Xelta makes professional-quality visuals and media accessible — without the complexity of traditional design tools.

As content demand continues to rise across social media, marketing, e-commerce, and branding, many teams struggle with slow production cycles and fragmented creative workflows. Xelta addresses this challenge by offering an all-in-one AI studio where users can generate visuals, design assets, and creative content instantly using natural language prompts.

“Technology should simplify creativity, not complicate it,” said Vishal Arya, CEO, MatchBest Group. “Xelta is our answer to the growing need for speed, quality, and accessibility in content creation. It empowers anyone - from solo creators to large teams - to bring ideas to life with confidence.”

Unlike conventional design platforms, Xelta focuses on intelligent creation rather than manual effort. Users can generate marketing creatives, product visuals, branded posters, magazine-style layouts, and AI-powered video content from a single workspace. The platform also includes smart editing tools and a flexible credit-based model that supports both occasional users and high-volume creators.

##### Xelta is designed to support:
- Digital marketing teams producing high-frequency content. 
- E-commerce brands building product visuals at scale
- Agencies managing multiple client campaigns
- Startups and founders building brand identity quickly
- Individual creators growing on social platforms

The launch of Xelta represents MatchBest Group’s continued commitment to building practical AI products that solve real business and creative challenges.

Xelta is now available globally.
Explore the platform at https://xelta.ai

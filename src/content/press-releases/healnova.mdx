##### HealNova Launches to Revolutionize Personal Wellness by Bringing AI-Powered Holistic Health Screenings to Every Smartphone.

[Gurugram, India] - [January, 2026] - MatchBest Group is proud to announce the official launch of HealNova, a groundbreaking new digital ecosystem designed to solve the lack of accessible, proactive health monitoring tools that bridge the gap between daily lifestyle and clinical diagnostics for health-conscious individuals seeking a smarter, data-driven way to manage their long-term wellness.

In an era where minor health concerns often go unnoticed until they become major issues and waiting for routine screenings can be time-consuming, HealNova provides a streamlined, intuitive solution that allows users to conduct instant wellness checks and receive personalized health insights using just their mobile camera with unprecedented ease.

"We built HealNova because we saw a massive gap in how individuals handle daily health tracking and preventive care," says Vishal Arya, CEO at MatchBest Group. "Our goal is to take the complexity out of understanding one's own body signals and give people back their time and peace of mind."

##### Key Features and Benefits:
- AI-Powered Visual Screening: 
HealNova uses advanced machine learning to analyze photos of the skin, eyes, nails, and tongue, providing instant wellness reports that identify potential imbalances before they escalate.
- Unified Holistic Dashboard: 
Beyond basic tracking, the platform integrates mental wellness support, pregnancy guidance, and personalized diet planning, creating a 360-degree view of the user’s health.
- Seamless Appointment Ecosystem: 
When professional care is needed, HealNova eliminates the friction of traditional booking by allowing users to find specialists and schedule consultations directly through the app.

##### Redefining the Standard
Unlike traditional tools that are often limited to simple step-counting or manual logging, HealNova focuses on predictive, image-based diagnostics and actionable wellness guidance. Within the first weeks of the beta rollout, users reported a significant increase in their awareness of daily health trends and a more proactive approach to lifestyle adjustments.

HealNova is available starting today at https://healnova.ai/.
